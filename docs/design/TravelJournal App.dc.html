TravelJournal · concept 1a

The trip as a trail you walk

Gamified-learning apps work because progress is a place: one lit node, everything before it earned, everything after it waiting. TravelJournal's day sheets already are that sequence — so the itinerary becomes a winding trail, packing becomes a quest with a reward, and the offline badge is a state you can feel rather than a warning. Warm Organic palette, chunky pressable buttons, springy motion. Tap anything: tabs, day stones, checklist rows, the expense sheet.
